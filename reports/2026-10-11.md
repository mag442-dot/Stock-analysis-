## 2. Top ideas (Keep)

**UCTT, Ultra Clean** (0.47 adj.)
- Thesis: EPS estimates +59% over 90 days and targets rising (Freedom initiated at 127 on 10-08) while the stock falls on an apparent semicap de-rating. 11.0x forward, PEG 0.35.
- Catalyst: Q3 earnings 2026-10-27 (Earnings Whispers shows 10-29, unverified). Guide $700–750M revenue.

**BMRN, BioMarin** (0.42–0.44 adj.)
