## 2. Top ideas (Keep)

**AVGO, Broadcom** (0.44 adj.)
- Thesis: The market is pricing lower margins on AI revenue growth. Analysts trimmed targets but stayed near-unanimous Buy (47/3/0), so the gap is partly stale and partly a real margin debate.
- Catalyst: FQ4 earnings 2026-12-09.
- Bear to monitor: Gross margin guided to ~74% from 77.1% on AI-system mix. Further compression with flat EPS, and VMware debt remains.

**AEIS, Advanced Energy** (0.265–0.270 adj.)
- Thesis: A growing business (Q2 revenue +30% y/y) trading below its recent peak. Targets predate the pullback, but PEG 2.77 limits the "cheap" label.
- Catalyst: Q3 earnings 2026-11-03 (guide $640M ±$20M).
- Bear to monitor: AI-hardware momentum after a 2026 gain above 170%. Q3 misses the guide midpoint, or the data-center growth guide (at least 50%) is cut. Balance sheet unverified.

**ADSK, Autodesk** (0.256–0.260 adj.)
- Thesis: A profitable, growing franchise de-rated on AI-disruption fears and a small guide miss. Analysts have not capitulated (8 raises vs 2 cuts in window).
- Catalyst: FQ3 earnings 2026-11-27.
- Bear to monitor: AI disruption of seat-based design software. Q3 EPS guide ($3.04–3.09) is below the $3.14 consensus, and the FCF guide was trimmed after MaintainX.

**AMAT, Applied Materials** (0.215–0.228 adj.)
- Thesis: Analysts did not cut and estimates are up, but 27x forward is not cheap on absolute terms. The gap looks like a modest sentiment discount rather than a clear mispricing.
- Catalyst: FQ4 earnings 2026-11-12.
- Bear to monitor: Cyclical WFE exposure and China export controls. WFE or China commentary that cuts FY27 estimates.
